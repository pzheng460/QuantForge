# Managed Covered Call Approximate Backtest

## Goal

Backtest the approved TSLA/NVDA daily options-management rules over the most
recent six months without representing modeled option quotes as historical
NBBO. Compare the managed overlay with buy-and-hold and the existing fixed
30-DTE covered-call approximation.

## Scope

The stateful simulation includes:

- 20/50/200-day moving averages, ATR14, 20-day return and realized volatility;
- the approved TrendScore classification;
- no new covered call in a strong uptrend or when downside review is required;
- 21–45 DTE selection and ticker/trend-specific target Delta;
- confirmed/estimated earnings buffers;
- liquidity and modeled spread gates;
- minimum core shares and maximum coverage ratio;
- 70% short-call profit taking;
- the approved dynamic Delta management threshold;
- hold, buy-to-close, roll-up and assignment comparisons;
- conservative buy-at-ask and sell-at-bid cash flows;
- explicit stock and option transaction costs.

Protective puts, collars and stock reductions are emitted as review signals but
are not executed. Historical option chains, dividends, taxes and early exercise
are unavailable and therefore are not fabricated.

## Data and quality

Daily stock OHLCV comes from Schwab. Option bid, ask and Greeks are generated by
the existing approximation pricer from rolling realized volatility. Earnings
dates are supplied as an explicit event calendar and sourced from company
Investor Relations where available.

Every result is labelled `approximate_unvalidated`. It cannot be labelled
`backtest_validated` because it has neither historical option NBBO nor an
out-of-sample validation period.

## State machine

Each ticker begins fully invested in whole shares. On every daily bar:

1. mark an open short call at modeled ask;
2. settle expiration/assignment consequences;
3. check 70% profit-taking and dynamic Delta management;
4. if management triggers, compare close-only and eligible roll-up candidates;
5. when flat, apply downside, trend, earnings, liquidity and coverage gates;
6. select the highest-scoring eligible call and sell at modeled bid;
7. record end-of-day equity, actions, costs and rejection reasons.

The simulator never creates more contracts than both coverage formulas allow.

## Verification

Contract tests cover:

- strong-uptrend and earnings-window entry blocks;
- core-share and maximum-coverage enforcement;
- profit taking and dynamic Delta management;
- explicit approximate quality label and deterministic results;
- transaction costs reducing equity;
- no executed put/collar trades.

The final report shows return, maximum drawdown, option P&L, turnover, action
counts and comparison with buy-and-hold and the fixed overlay.
